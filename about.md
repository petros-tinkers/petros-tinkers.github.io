My name is **Peter**, or *Petros* in my native Greek. 

I’m a Mechatronics Tech with a passion for building and fixing things. I love solving problems that don't have obvious answers, diving deep into issues instead of throwing parts at them.

To do that I combine systematic diagnosis with custom solutions, often involving CAD/3D printing and designing electronics to replace faulty factory components.

This website is where I document my projects. Explaining helps me learn (plus, I love connecting with people). I work primarily on my own vehicles/projects, which allows me to experiment freely and really understand the systems.
